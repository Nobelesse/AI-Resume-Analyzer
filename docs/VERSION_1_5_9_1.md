# Version 1.5.9.1 — Job Studio Scoring Repair

Title-only comparisons use role-skill alignment; a two-word job title is never scored against the entire resume via cosine similarity. Required/preferred sections are omitted until a job description is supplied. With a JD, explicit requirement evidence and skill overlap are assessed. Unverifiable qualifications remain for manual review. Progress indicators replace the unstable chart. PDF/HTML reports describe the active mode. Scores remain heuristic; no employer ATS validation is implied.
