# Version 1.5.7.1 — Career Finder Reliability Fix

Adds accounting, customer service, administrative, IT support and entry-level web domains. Filters weak, incidental occupation matches rather than presenting unrelated careers to freshers. This is a conservative heuristic, not an employment eligibility verdict. Existing resume uploads and local data remain private and outside the distribution.

Known limitation: low-evidence users may see no suggestions; this is preferable to unsupported recommendations. The engine does not certify degrees, seniority or experience, and career categories are not exhaustive.
